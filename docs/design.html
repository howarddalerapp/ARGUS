Project Plan — ARGUS

Executive summary, phases, schedule, ROI — extracted from ARGUS_Project_Plan.md.

Project Summary

  • Community: River Shoals HOA (280+ lots)
  • Objective: Replace CPS vendor-managed security with board-owned UniFi infrastructure
  • Equipment budget: ~$8,305 (≈$8,886 with tax)
  • Operating cost: ~$50–70/mo (LTE SIMs at gates)

Phases (high level)

  1. Phase 1 — Clubhouse foundation: cameras, UNVR, PoE switch, WiFi (Aug)
  2. Phase 2 — Pool & door access: UA-Hub, readers, fob issuance (Aug)
  3. Phase 3 — Gates: LTE Pro, LPR cameras, intercom, cutover (Sep)

Schedule & ROI

Break-even vs CPS occurs rapidly — a single large CPS invoice can justify the project. Detailed schedule and gantt charts are in the source file.
